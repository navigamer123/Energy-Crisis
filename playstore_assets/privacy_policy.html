Privacy Policy for Energy Crisis

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Energy Crisis ("we", "our", or "the Game") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application on Android.

1. Information We Collect

Energy Crisis does not require user accounts, logins, or personal names/emails to play.

Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The Game integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device to deliver advertisements:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, coarse location, and app performance or analytics data to provide personalized and non-personalized advertising in compliance with Google's policies.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please visit: Google's Privacy & Terms.

2. Game Modes & Rewarded Advertising

Energy Crisis offers two primary modes:

  • 5-Minute Blitz Mode: Completely free and unlimited with no advertisements required.
  • 30-Minute Battle Mode: May offer optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob, which players may choose to watch to unlock extended gameplay sessions.

3. Children's Privacy

Energy Crisis is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

4. Data Security

We do not operate external backend servers storing player personal data. All local settings (e.g. sound volume, language preferences, high scores) are stored locally on your device.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer contact page or support channel on Google Play.
