Power the metropolis. Outsmart rival energy barons. Survive the elements.

• Hydro Plants, Coal & Gas Power Stations (reliable baseline energy)
• Advanced Battery Storage (store surplus power to survive harsh night blackouts)
• Research Labs & High-Voltage Transformers

🌪️ DYNAMIC WEATHER & DISASTERS:
Mother Nature won't make it easy. Adapt your production to changing seasons, freezing winter cold snaps, and devastating storms that test the resilience of your grid.

🤖 SMART AI & VERSATILE CONTROLS:

• Intuitive touch controls designed specifically for mobile play.
